The weekly cadence (core loop)

Thursday
Leo sync. Review last week's posted performance, lock next week's recording list, Leo flags needs.
Mon / Tue
Chris records. Films the week's concepts.
Wednesday
Chris shares files. Named, organized, handed to Leo.
Following week
Leo edits, captions, schedules, posts to Meta. ~1 week turnaround.

2–3 batches always in flight: this week recording / last week editing / prior week posting.

Repurposing chains

One recording becomes many posts, automatically. Tag a card with a chain and, when it's recorded, the repurposed pieces are created for you and linked back to the original. It turns Chris's single recording block into the origination point for LinkedIn, IG cuts, and the newsletter, so those lanes fill without anyone remembering to make them.

  • How to use it: open a card, pick a chain under Repurposing chain, save. That's the only step.
  • What happens: when the card first hits Recorded & shared with editor (or Design, for lead magnets), the derivatives appear in Planned with a 🔗 link back to the parent, and Slack posts one summary.
  • The chains: Pillar video → LinkedIn post + IG cut + newsletter section · Case study → LinkedIn post + IG cut + partner asset · Lead magnet launch → giveaway post.
  • Opt-in: no chain means nothing changes and the card behaves normally. Use it only when you actually plan to repurpose that piece.
  • The parent shows a ⛓ badge + a Derivatives list; children show "from [parent]". Deleting a parent keeps the children, just unlinked.

Roles & handoffs

  • Sarah — owns the pipeline. Runs Thursday sync, keeps assets moving.
  • Chris — records (Mon/Tue), shares raw files (Wed). On-camera + creative voice.
  • Leo — edits, captions, schedules, posts. Owns most ongoing creative from the bank.
  • One clear owner per stage. No ambiguous handoffs.
  • File naming at Chris→Leo: track-concept-##-YYYYMMDD (track = gtm / cs / vsl).
  • "Recorded" isn't done until files are named + dropped in the agreed location.

Production specs & testing

  • Vertical short-form for Meta, 15–30s, native / raw (phone-in-office > studio).
  • Hook captioned on-screen in frame 1 (sound-off). One idea per ad.
  • CTA per track: CS = "free CS audit", GTM = "free outbound audit / book your read". The call is "the read".
  • One concept per ad set, multiple hook variants inside it. Isolate the variable — only the first 3 sec change.
  • Judge hooks on 3-sec hold rate first, CTR second.
